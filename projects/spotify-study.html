USER EXPERIENCE DESIGN · SEM 4

Spotify UX Laws and Principles

Analysing Spotify's interface through usability principles, navigation and information organisation.

Project Overview

This study examines the Spotify app to understand how interface design and usability principles shape the music-listening experience.

UX Analysis

The analysis explores visual hierarchy, navigation, information organisation and the application of usability laws and design principles.

Key Observations

The study considers how interface elements guide users, support content discovery and make common actions easier to understand.

Outcome

The project builds an understanding of how UX principles can be used to evaluate an existing digital product and identify opportunities for improvement.
