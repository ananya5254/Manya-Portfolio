Himalayan Packaging

Exploring the relationship between nature, material and premium packaging through a Himalayan-inspired body mist packaging concept.

Project Overview

This project explores packaging inspired by the natural beauty of the Himalayas. The design focuses on translating the essence of natural ingredients, organic forms and earthy textures into a premium packaging experience.

The Concept

Inspired by the textures of stones and the beauty of Himalayan flowers, the concept brings together natural elements and a refined visual identity. The packaging aims to create a connection between the product and its natural origins.

Design Exploration

The exploration focuses on bottle form, material textures, colour variations and outer carton design. Natural textures and flower-inspired details help distinguish the different variants while maintaining a consistent design language.

Final Outcome

The final concept presents a nature-inspired body mist packaging system that combines organic visual elements with a premium and contemporary appearance.
