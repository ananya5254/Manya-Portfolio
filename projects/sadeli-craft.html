PRODUCT DESIGN · SEM 5

Sadeli Craft

Exploring the heritage of Sadeli, a traditional geometric inlay craft, and its relevance in contemporary product design.

Project Overview

This project documents the Sadeli craft, its geometric patterns, materials and traditional making practices. It explores how this heritage craft can be appreciated and carried forward through contemporary design.

Craft Exploration

The study focuses on the craft's repetitive geometric patterns, material combinations and the skill involved in creating detailed inlay work.

Design Outcome

The project explores a contemporary product inspired by Sadeli's distinctive patterns while retaining a connection to the traditional craft.
